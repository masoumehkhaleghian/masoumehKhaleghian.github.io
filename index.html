  • AI / Deep Learning Engineer
  • Developed a deep learning framework for non-invasive and cuffless blood pressure estimation directly from photoplethysmography (PPG) signals, enabling continuous arterial blood pressure (ABP) waveform reconstruction and estimation of systolic, diastolic, and mean arterial pressure.
  • Implemented a signal-to-signal regression architecture based on Deep Supervised Multi-Residual U-Net (DSMR-UNet), combining encoder-decoder learning, skip connections, multi-residual blocks, residual paths, and deep supervision for accurate PPG-to-ABP waveform translation.

  • Evaluated model generalization on an independently collected PPG-BP dataset containing signals from 270 participants, investigating cross-dataset performance and the effects of motion artifacts and domain shift on blood pressure estimation.
  • Conducted the project as applied AI research at the Iranian Research Organization for Science and Technology (IROST), with the resulting methodology and experimental evaluation documented in the research paper “AI-Based Blood Pressure Estimation from Photoplethysmography Signals to Develop Medical Monitoring Devices' Capabilities.”
